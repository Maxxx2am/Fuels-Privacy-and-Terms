# Fuels — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 8, 2026**

Fuels ("we", "our", or "the app") is built to keep your data yours. This policy explains what Fuels collects, what it does not, and how third-party services fit in.

## The short version

- Your food log, weight, body metrics, and settings live **only on your device**.
- We **do not** operate any servers, accounts, or analytics. There is nothing to log in to, and nothing to leak.
- When you use AI features, the content you submit (photos, text, voice transcripts) is sent **directly from your device to Google's Gemini API** using the API key you provide. We never see it.
- Deleting the app deletes everything.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Fuels has no backend, no accounts, no sign-up, and no telemetry. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information, identifiers, or usage data.

## What stays on your device

The following is stored locally on your device (or in Apple Health, if you connect it) and nowhere else:

- Food entries, meals, and photos you add
- Weight, body measurements, and water logs
- Workout history and fasting timers
- Your profile, goals, and preferences
- Your Gemini API key (stored in the device Keychain)

## What is sent to Google

AI features (food photo analysis, text meal entry, voice logging, Coach chat) require the Google Gemini API. When you use them:

- Requests go **directly from your device to Google**, authenticated with your own key. Fuels cannot read or intercept them.
- Your use of Gemini is additionally governed by [Google's Terms of Service](https://ai.google.dev/terms) and its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Review them before use.
- If you never use AI features, nothing is ever sent to anyone.

## Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Fuels reads and writes only the data you permit (nutrition, weight, body measurements). This sync happens entirely on-device between apps. You can disconnect at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

## Children

Fuels is not directed at children under 13, and we collect no data from anyone — including children.

## Your rights and choices

- **Access/erase:** all data is on your device; delete entries in-app or uninstall to erase everything.
- **No tracking:** the app contains no ad SDKs, no analytics, and no third-party trackers of any kind.
- **AI data:** you control the key and can stop AI use at any time by removing it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted in this repository with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
